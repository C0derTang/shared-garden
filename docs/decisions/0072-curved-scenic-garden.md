# 0072 — Curved garden with scenic stops

Approved 2026-11-26; implements [issue #224](https://github.com/C0derTang/shared-garden/issues/224).

The user approved a visibly winding looping garden path, changing flower/lake views, an actual rear view of the landing house, and click/tap navigation between **set scenic stops**. Existing flower activities return to the selected scenic view. Keyboard and screen-reader activation reach the same stops. GardenV2 no longer uses WASD/arrows, a directional pad or arbitrary ground destinations. This supersedes presentation/navigation in [0070](0070-flower-type-areas.md) and [0071](0071-free-garden-walking.md), retaining their data/activity/privacy rules. [0067](0067-garden-overhaul.md), [0004](0004-finalized-launch-rules.md) and [0069](0069-measured-kuwahara-optimization.md) remain applicable.

Conservative implementation choices under that approved discretion:

- Thirteen named planting pockets flank the eastern winding portion of a closed route. Three additional scenic stops complete the loop: the meadow bend, the house rear and the lakeside. Main pockets follow a gentle sinusoidal curve, separated by 4.2 metres in depth; the return winds around the west of the house. Cameras look toward their pocket, then turn to the meadow, actual rear wall/gable and lake on the return. The same cubic Bézier edges supply the visible earth/gravel ribbon and camera traversal.
- A type's persisted twelve-address section has an immutable position. Section zero is always present; additional occupied/next-planting sections extend east along the type's fixed row, with a parallel access spur on their near side. Only used sections and their direct connecting edges are generated. Existing addresses never compact when holes are filled. This repositions presentation once without rewriting any IDs, spots, seeds, growth, history or media.
- Each stop has a stable string ID, label and pose. A persistent compact back/current/onward control and a labelled native stop selector remain visible and operable even when projected route markers are behind the camera. World markers select route stops, never flower plots. Flower/seed picking and ordinary UI retain priority. Dragging does not navigate.
- Navigation uses the connected route graph, follows its curves in either direction, and interpolates view direction along the shortest angular arc. Rapid requests replan from the current point on the same edge; they never cut across the world. Travel is eased, with bounded segment duration. Reduced motion selects the destination directly. Interruptions cancel travel coherently at its selected destination, preventing stale motion on resume.
- The original house mesh and materials are reused through an explicit rigid garden transform: house-local front points east, with the rear wall facing the western scenic stop. Its original scale/proportions, chimney, siding, roof and foundation remain intact; landing geometry, frame and door behavior retain their defaults. House geometry participates in existing scene, shadow and reflection passes.
- The selected stop is local React state only, retained during flower close-up/return and GPU recovery. Arrival settles into the first flower view. Overlays, fields, close-up/return and arrival block navigation. Responsive controls respect safe areas and reserve space above the first-use hint.

No avatar, persisted/shared location, house interior, new activity rule, schema/backend change, production fixture, service or cost is introduced. Synthetic actual-UI evidence is development-only and must remain 404 in production. Verification and limitations are recorded with the feature PR; fresh independent exact-head review and all required CI remain merge gates.
