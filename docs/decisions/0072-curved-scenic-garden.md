## Conservative layout and interaction choices

- Fit two compact rounded planted islands in the original dry inland meadow strip, east of the near forest and south of the distant forest. Starting source-frame fit: world centres around `(x=18,z=60)` and `(x=18,z=102)`, with roughly 8 metres in world x and 15 metres in world z as ellipse radii. Exact local adjustments follow measured full ribbon/camera width, organic pocket footprints and original trunk/rock clearance. A curved woodland connection threads existing gaps from the actual rear porch steps. Original trees stay at their original positions. Six type pockets are inside loops and seven on the outskirts; all thirteen type groupings remain available, including when empty. A composed moderate raised oblique overlook is the first/arrival stop; direct approaches and varied inward/outward headings reveal nearby plants, curved arcs and original scenery. A natural near-house gap supplies the rear view; no tree removal is an acceptable framing fix.
- Additional occupied/next-planting twelve-address sections use bounded rounded meadow loops farther inland, outside original landmark footprints (starting beyond world z≈150), rather than a ladder of parallel spurs or extensions into water. Generate only used sections and their shared access curves, with bounded work per section and no allocation for missing address gaps. Type/section frames and IDs remain deterministic: this is an unshipped presentation remap, without rewriting original IDs, spots, seeds, growth, history, media or activity state. Later expansion and lower-hole filling preserve planted positions.
- A pure `ScenicRoute` contract owns islands, stable stop IDs and shared cubic edges. Path ribbon and camera x/z traversal consume those same curves, with rounded/tangent-matched loop joins. Primary width is 1.44 metres; organic soil rims, sparse edging stones and low groundcover soften pockets. `scenicPose`/`scenicFovY` supply the exact rendered aspect-aware pose/lens to picking tests as well as rendering. Camera footprint and path half-width are included in real-solid clearance checks.
- Graph navigation follows curves in either direction, uses shortest-angle turns, and retargets from the current edge coordinate. Reduced motion selects the destination directly. Blur/visibility/overlay interruptions settle coherently; removed pending-only stops fall back to the relevant stable type view. Latest selection is reconciled after renderer focus reset, including a React selection received before the next rendered frame, preventing a dropped close-return selection.
- Persistent Back/current/Onward controls plus a labelled native selector remain reachable when projected markers are behind the camera. Previous/next plus up to six nearby existing fixed stops supply world-marker candidates. Fully clipped/covered markers are hidden while keyboard names remain available; markers avoid actual navigation rectangles. Flower/seed/UI targets retain priority. Captured out-and-back pointer excursions and cancellation do not become clicks; native selector semantics remain intact.
- Selected stop is local React state retained through flower close-up/return and GPU recovery. Actual renderer readiness gates close-return controls. Arrival, full-screen/scoped sheets and fields block scene navigation. Existing accessible plot navigation, care hearts, planting seeds, history/media/privacy/activity behavior and lifecycle/recovery remain intact.
- Original terrain/shoreline outputs are shared without changing values. Garden-only ground coverage follows the same analytic site and keeps path/soil above all composed ground layers, including bounded sparse corridors; no depth-always workaround ships. A collectable per-route nearest-segment spatial index accelerates meadow clearances, with brute-force parity tests and separate CPU/GPU evidence. Original quality/resolution budgets and optimized/reference shaders are preserved.
