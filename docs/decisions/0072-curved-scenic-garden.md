# 0072 — Curved garden with scenic stops

Approved 2026-11-26; implements [issue #224](https://github.com/C0derTang/shared-garden/issues/224).

The user approved a visibly winding looping garden path, changing flower/lake views, an actual rear view of the landing house, and click/tap navigation between **set scenic stops**. Existing flower activities return to the selected scenic view. Keyboard and screen-reader activation reach the same stops. GardenV2 no longer uses WASD/arrows, a directional pad or arbitrary ground destinations. This supersedes presentation/navigation in [0070](0070-flower-type-areas.md) and [0071](0071-free-garden-walking.md), retaining their data/activity/privacy rules. [0067](0067-garden-overhaul.md), [0004](0004-finalized-launch-rules.md) and [0069](0069-measured-kuwahara-optimization.md) remain applicable.

## Approved amendment

During the active build, the user requested an actual back door and porch, and rounded paths with planted islands and flowers on the outskirts. [Approval record](https://github.com/C0derTang/shared-garden/issues/224#issuecomment-7206460284). This replaces the initial unshipped parallel-spur/blank-rear composition. A subsequent [geographic clarification](https://github.com/C0derTang/shared-garden/issues/224#issuecomment-7206664458) requires front and rear to share the same physical site, original house frame, actual shoreline/terrain/lake, sun/cardinal axes and landmark positions; no house relocation/rotation or separate garden lake is permitted. The back door, covered rear porch and steps are modeled garden-only additions; no entry action or interior is introduced.

Conservative implementation choices under the approved discretion:

- Three organic green islands have separate closed rounded loops, connected through curved meadow gates. Type pockets are assigned five/four/four across those islands; six are inside the loops and seven along the outskirts, distributed on both sides. Inward/outward scenic cameras face different compositions. A lakeside stop belongs to the first loop and an oblique rear-house view to the final loop. Primary loop centres lie behind the actual rear in a shared backyard frame, roughly31metres inland and26/78/126metres deeper behind it, with differing horizontal/depth radii around17–21metres; exact sampled control points are the shared path/camera authority. Three raised island-overlook stops interleave with type approaches; the initial/arrival view is the first island overlook, making rounded structure visible immediately. Camera height/lens interpolate during route travel.
- Each type owns an immutable row/side frame. Section zero is its island/outskirt pocket. Additional occupied/next-planting twelve-address sections1–2 expand into nearby rounded satellite-pocket loops on that side, separated by seven metres and connected along their clear near side by curved links. To keep unbounded westward addresses out of the actual lake, sections3+ use deterministic inland back-meadow annex columns/levels and a curved clear-side spine. Only occupied/next levels and their pockets are built; sparse addresses never allocate all missing levels. Only used sections and their connecting curves are generated; no missing-address cells or satellite loops are allocated. Satellite loops surround actual pockets and remain visually secondary to the three primary islands. This is one unshipped presentation remap, without rewriting IDs, spots, seeds, growth, history or media. Future expansion and filling lower holes preserve planted positions.
- `ScenicIsland`/type frames, shared cubic edges, stable stop IDs and one navigator provide the geometry contract. Closed-loop edges meet with matching tangents. The visible path ribbon and camera traversal consume those same curves. Main path width is about1.3metres; secondary access is quieter. Organic soil rims, sparse small edging stones and low groundcover soften planting pockets.
- A persistent back/current/onward control and labelled native stop selector stay reachable when projected route markers are behind the camera. World markers select route stops, never flower plots. Flower/seed picking and ordinary UI retain priority; captured pointer excursions cannot become accidental clicks. Native select touch/keyboard semantics remain intact.
- Connected-graph navigation traverses curves in either direction and uses shortest-angle view interpolation. Rapid selection replans from the same current edge coordinate. Travel is eased with bounded segment duration. Reduced motion selects the destination directly. Interruptions settle coherently at the selected destination; a removed pending-only stop falls back to that type's stable first view, without stale travel.
- The original landing `houseToWorld`/`worldToHouse`, `HOUSE` and `FRONT_NORMAL` remain authoritative. The same complete house mesh, with original world vertices/normals and the added rear porch, is used in both actual scenes. The front facade, proportions, door anchor and entry animation remain intact. Rear door uses its own static material so opening the front cannot swing the back door. Rear additions are a closed red plank door/cream trim, modest covered timber porch/posts/rails and three steps, built with the existing materials/mesh code. They add no entry action or interior. The path visibly reaches the true rear steps and curves into the island route.
- A tiny pure shared-site module preserves the existing landing terrain/shoreline/lake outputs; scenic water uses its actual shoreline mask, water level and extents. Backyard axes derive from the actual rear centre and existing design/world cardinal basis, with the garden on the rear half-plane and dry inland ground. Source-frame/landmark tests and current front/rear/top-down renders verify geographic continuity. Shared trees/rocks retain their site positions; backyard clearing needed for the garden is coordinated in both views. DOM labels are occluded in the true house-local footprint; distant/colliding labels are culled while keyboard names remain available.
- Selected stop is local React state, retained during flower close-up/return and GPU recovery. Arrival settles into the first scenic view. Actual renderer readiness gates controls through close-up return. Overlays, scoped sheets, fields and arrival block navigation. Controls respect safe areas at all supported sizes and reserve space above the first-use hint.

No avatar, persisted/shared location, house interior, new activity rule, schema/backend change, production fixture, service or cost is introduced. Synthetic actual-UI evidence is development-only and must remain 404 in production. Verification and limitations are recorded with the feature PR; fresh independent exact-head review and all required CI remain merge gates.
