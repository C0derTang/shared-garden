# 0062 — Marigold keeps daily care after bloom

Status: approved (owner request, [issue #155](https://github.com/C0derTang/shared-garden/issues/155), 2026-10-06).
Amends decision 0004 for Marigold only.

## Decision

A bloomed Marigold stays part of daily care, the way the Cactus does. Both
members are expected to write a compliment each garden day. It appears in
Today's to-do and the garden surface cues, and a paired day is qualifying
activity for streaks.

- One original entry per member per garden day; the existing 30-minute
  author-only edit window applies.
- Rollover records a day fact for each bloomed Marigold. Growth stays at the
  bloom target; a missed day never reduces it.
- No at-risk cue applies, since growth cannot fall. Partner-cared and
  cared-together cues apply as for any care flower.
- Before-noon snapshots keep excluding bloomed flowers, as for the bloomed
  Cactus. Ten-minute timing credit applies to its pairs like any other pair.
- Unbloomed Marigold behavior, the two-growing limit, the ten-Marigolds
  achievement and every other species are unchanged. Other bloomed ordinary
  flowers remain closed.

## Why

The owner wants the daily compliment habit to continue after the flower
blooms, as required care rather than an optional form.
